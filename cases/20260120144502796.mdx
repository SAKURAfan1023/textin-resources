SPDB Financial Leasing is a financial institution established with Shanghai Pudong Development Bank as its principal founding shareholder. It provides financial leasing services across industries including aviation, shipping, and infrastructure.

As the business expanded, so did the volume of procurement, purchase-and-sale, and leasing contracts. Processing approximately 200,000 contracts annually through manual review and data entry was becoming increasingly difficult to sustain.

## The work behind 200,000 contracts

For every contract, employees had to review the document, locate relevant information, extract key terms, and enter the data into internal business systems.

The growing workload created an increasingly serious bottleneck. Complex layouts and specialized leasing terminology made manual extraction time-consuming, while missing or incorrect information could introduce downstream risks. Errors involving leased assets, transaction amounts, contract dates, or contractual rights and obligations could affect financial operations, legal compliance, and risk management.

Simply adding more staff to handle the increasing volume would raise operating costs without addressing the underlying processing problem.

## Extracting the terms that drive leasing operations

SPDB Financial Leasing selected INTSIG DocFlow after its technology and business teams evaluated the available solutions.

DocFlow is optimized for the complex layouts and specialized terminology found in financial leasing contracts. It identifies key information in context, including leased asset details, transaction amounts, contract durations, and the rights and obligations of each party.

Rather than requiring employees to locate and enter each field individually, DocFlow extracts key contract information in batches and converts it into structured data for downstream business applications.

![A contract and its extracted fields in the original DocFlow interface](/images/cases/20260120144502796/figure-1.webp)

## Connecting extracted data to core business systems

Document extraction only becomes useful at scale when the results can reach the systems that rely on them.

DocFlow provides API services that integrate with SPDB Financial Leasing's internal core business applications. Contracts are uploaded, parsed, and processed automatically, with extracted information returned to the relevant systems.

The platform supports daily batches of hundreds to more than a thousand contracts in the described operating environment. Its concurrent processing capabilities also support continuous, around-the-clock operations as demand fluctuates.

This creates a connected workflow from contract upload and information extraction to data delivery, reducing the need for separate manual processing before contract information can enter business operations.

## More contracts, without the same increase in manual work

The scale of the deployment is reflected in three reported indicators:

- **Approximately 200,000 contracts:** Annual contract workload.
- **More than 1.45 million:** Cumulative system calls.
- **Broadly stable:** Back-office staffing as business volumes grew.

Automating key information extraction has shortened contract processing cycles and reduced repetitive manual review and data entry. More standardized extracted information also provides a stronger foundation for internal management and risk control.

For a financial leasing business handling hundreds of thousands of contracts, the value goes beyond processing speed. By connecting document extraction directly with core business systems, SPDB Financial Leasing can support greater contract volumes without relying on manual capacity to grow at the same rate.
